Rules for Shooting at High Shutter Speeds
1

Move VERY slowly: Lock the camera off or slide gently. Sharp pans trigger severe judder above 1/125.

2

Gentle Subject Action: Avoid rapid cross-frame walks; they look artificial and choppy.

3

The Slow-Mo Hack: Record 50p at 1/1000. Slowing it down to 25p in post entirely hides high-shutter stutter!

4

Maintain Match: Keep shutter consistent within a scene. Don't mix 1/50 and 1/1000 in cuts.

Stabilization Protocols

IBIS ON
Handheld Static Moves

Two hands, elbows in, exhale, hold 8–10 seconds. Enjoy full-frame 5-axis sensor stabilization.

e-VR ON
Walking & Tracking Shots

Enables advanced digital stabilization. Adds a minor 1.1× crop, so walk with bent knees.

IBIS OFF
Tripod / Locked-Off Work

Always turn stabilization OFF on stable tripods. Sensor can drift in place trying to stabilize static air.

Color Exposure Discipline

SDR / Flat (Daily Sun) Base ISO 100

Set Contrast −2, Saturation −1, Sharpening +1. Expose so skin is 60–70 IRE on the waveform, zebras trigger at 95% highlight clipping.

N-Log (Controlled Interiors) Base ISO 800

Expose to the Right (ETTR) by 1 to 1.5 stops to keep shadows noise-free. Avoid N-Log outdoors in direct sun without heavy ND filters.

Pro Cinematic Habits

10-Second Handles: Record for 10s minimum per shot. Editors need ample pad room.
One Move rule: Pan OR slide in, never blend complex wild directions in one frame.
Foreground Layers: Shoot through glass, leaves, arches to add dimensional depth.
Backlight Strategy: Frame sun/windows behind subject. Free negative fill and high dynamic protection.

Zf Hardware Constraints & Warnings

Micro-SD Card Warning

The MicroSD slot 2 is strictly too slow for 4K video recording. Keep all 4K files bound to full-size UHS-II SD cards in slot 1 only.

Micro-HDMI Fragility

Type-D micro-HDMI output ports are delicate on set. Strongly recommend a custom rig cage or clamps if using external field monitors.

Rolling Shutter Notice

Avoid lightning-fast whip-pans and hard vertical lines during fast lateral slides. Zf oversampled 6K sensor is superb but prone to minor rolling lag.

Power Management

Zf EN-EL15c batteries deplete fast in 4K. Plan for 3+ spares on-set, or utilize USB-C Power Delivery banks for static setups.
