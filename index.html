Frame rate first. Then shutter.

For normal filming: 25p → 1/50. If sunshine is too bright, find shade first; then close aperture; then use a faster shutter.

Keep it clean

Night, focus & movement

Most bad night footage comes from underexposure, hunting focus, or too much movement—not from using a high ISO.

Night shooting

  • At 25p, keep 1/50 unless everything is static.
  • Open to f/2 or f/2.8, then raise ISO.
  • Start at 3500–4500K for neon/shop lights.
  • Put faces near a shop window, lamp, or small LED.
  • Use SDR/Flat for easy run-and-gun work. Do not underexpose N-Log.

Focus & stability

  • People: AF-C + People detection + Wide-area L.
  • Set movie AF speed around −3 to −5 for gentle transitions.
  • Products / dark scenes: manual focus + peaking.
  • Handheld: IBIS on. Tripod: IBIS and e-VR off.
  • At f/2, give moving people room; f/2.8–f/4 is safer.

Protect the image

  • SDR/Flat: zebras at 95%.
  • Set white balance manually before each scene—never Auto WB mid-shot.
  • Check waveform; protect faces and important highlights.
  • Record audio manually, dialogue peaking around −12 dB.

Make it feel cinematic

  • Hold each usable shot for at least 10 seconds.
  • One movement per shot: pan or push-in.
  • Take wide, medium, close and detail coverage.
  • At 40mm, brace yourself and pan very slowly.
