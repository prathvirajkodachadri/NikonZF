THE NO-ND DOCTRINE

When sunlight is
too bright, choose.

Without an ND filter, light management replaces filtration. Work through this ladder in order.

  1. 1
    Change whenGolden hour, blue hour, or overcast are free ND.
  2. 2
    Change whereOpen shade drops light by 3–4 stops.
  3. 3
    Stop downf/8–f/16 keeps the 1/50 shutter alive.
  4. 4
    Raise shutterIn direct sun, 1/500 at f/8 is the balanced choice.
  5. 5
    Shoot 50pConform to 25p for graceful slow motion.
0125p → 1/50

Natural motion. Under 50 Hz artificial light, stay at 1/50 or 1/100.

02Base ISO first

03f/2 to f/16

04Day: protect highlights

Don’t get caught

  • 3+ EN-EL15c batteries
  • USB-C PD bank for static shoots.
  • Protect micro HDMI with a cage/clamp.
FOCUS

Focus first, then roll.

  1. Use AF-C + People/Auto for subjects.
  2. Set AF speed to −5 and tracking sensitivity to 4–5.
  3. For critical shots, use MF + peaking + punch-in, then lock.
  4. At night, pre-focus with a phone torch and lock it.
  5. The 40mm focuses as close as 0.29 m; do not push closer.

AUDIO

Record sound on purpose.

  • Internal mic is sync/reference only.
  • Use a shotgun or wireless lav for dialogue.
  • Manual level: dialogue peaks at −12 dB.
  • Use a wind muff outside — always.
  • Capture 10 seconds of room tone at every location.

QUICK REMINDER

Exposure triangle
made simple.

Exposure means how bright your photo or video looks. These three settings work like a team: change one, and you may need to balance another.

EXPOSURE brightness
A

Aperture

Lens opening
Low f-number = more light + blurry background.

S

Shutter

Time light enters
Slower = brighter + more motion blur.

I

ISO

Camera brightness boost
Higher = brighter + more grain/noise.

01

Want blurry background?

Use f/2 or f/2.8. If it becomes too bright, lower ISO or use a faster shutter.

02

Want natural video motion?

Use 25p with 1/50. This is the easiest cinematic starting point in India.

03

Scene is too dark?

First open aperture, then add light, then raise ISO only as much as needed.
