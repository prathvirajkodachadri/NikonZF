Zf ZF Field Companion — Nikon Zf Settings Guide

DIAGNOSER

Shot looks bad?
Find the fix.

TOO BLURRY

Photo looks soft or shaky.

Raise shutter to 1/125 or faster. Check IBIS is on for handheld. Confirm focus locked before shooting.

TOO DARK

Shadows are crushed black.

Open the aperture (smaller f-number). Raise ISO. Use + on the exposure comp dial.

TOO BRIGHT

Highlights are blown out.

Use − on the exposure comp dial. Stop down to f/8 in bright sun. Lower ISO to base 100.

WRONG COLOUR

Photo looks too warm or too blue.

Set Kelvin manually — 5600K outdoors, 3200K warm indoor. Turn Auto WB off.

FOCUS HUNTING

Camera keeps searching for focus.

Switch to AF-S for still subjects, AF-C for moving. Use single-point or wide-area, not auto-area.

NOISY / GRAINY

Picture looks speckled in shadow.

Lower ISO if light allows. Use the 24-120 at f/4 in low light instead of pushing ISO on the 40mm.
